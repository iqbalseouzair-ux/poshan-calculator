# Poshan Calculator – Child Growth and Nutrition Assessment

Poshan Calculator is an educational online tool designed to help parents, caregivers, and health workers better understand child growth and nutritional indicators.

## About Child Growth Monitoring

Child growth monitoring involves observing a child's development using measurements such as age, height, and weight. These measurements can help identify potential growth concerns and support nutritional assessment.

## Growth Indicators

- **Height-for-Age (HFA):** Helps assess linear growth and identify potential stunting.
- **Weight-for-Age (WFA):** Helps assess weight relative to age and identify underweight.
- **Weight-for-Height (WFH):** Helps assess weight relative to height and identify wasting.
- **BMI-for-Age:** Helps evaluate body mass index relative to age.
- **MUAC:** Mid-Upper Arm Circumference is used for nutritional screening.
